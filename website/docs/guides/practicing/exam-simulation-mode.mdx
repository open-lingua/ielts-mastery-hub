# How to Structure Your Own Full Practice Exam

Many students want to practise the full exam experience, not just one module at a time. In the app today, the usual way to do that is to take **Reading, Listening, and Writing back-to-back**.

:::note
There is **no single "Exam Simulation" button or separate combined exam page** in the app at the moment. Instead, you start each module separately from the Practice Library.
:::

## A simple full-mock plan

| Stage | Module | Typical app timing |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **Reading** | 60 minutes |
| 2 | **Listening** | section-based test with its own timer |
| 3 | **Writing** | 60 minutes + AI grading at the end |

If you want a realistic session, set aside about **2.5 hours**, plus a few minutes to move from one module to the next.

## How students usually do it in this app

1. Open the **Practice Library**.
2. Choose one Reading test, one Listening test, and one Writing test.
3. Start the first module and complete it under timed conditions.
4. Return to the library and open the next module straight away.
5. Finish all three in one sitting.

## Tips for making it feel more like exam day

- study in a quiet place
- put your phone away
- keep another browser tab or other apps closed if possible
- use headphones for Listening
- move straight into the next module instead of taking a long break
- avoid checking notes during the test

:::tip
Prepare your tests before you begin so you are not browsing the library halfway through your mock exam.
:::

## What to expect from results

Because the modules are separate, you will also get **separate results**:

- Reading gives its own band score and answer review
- Listening gives its own band score and section breakdown
- Writing gives its own AI feedback report

There is currently **no single combined overall mock-exam score screen** inside the app.

## Internet and offline use

You can do most of a full mock exam offline.

- **Reading** works offline
- **Listening** works offline
- **Writing AI grading** needs internet

So if you want the full Writing feedback report at the end, make sure you are online before you submit your writing.

## A practical way to track yourself

After each module, note down:

- your band score
- how confident you felt
- which question types slowed you down
- whether timing was a problem

That gives you a simple record of how close your full practice session felt to the real exam.
