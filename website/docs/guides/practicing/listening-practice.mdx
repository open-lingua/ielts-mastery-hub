# How to Practice Listening

The Listening module lets you work through a full test one section at a time. Each section has its own audio, instructions, and question set.

## What the test looks like

A full listening test is split into **4 sections**.

At the start, you will see:

- the test title
- the number of sections
- the total number of questions
- the test time limit shown at the top

Click **Start Now** when you are ready.

## How the section flow works

At the top of the screen, you will see a **section stepper**.

It helps you track:

- which section you are on now
- which sections are already completed
- which sections are still locked

You move forward one section at a time. When you submit the current section, the next section unlocks.

## Using the audio player

Each section includes an audio player with simple controls:

- **play / pause**
- a **progress bar**
- **volume** control
- an **ended** state when the track finishes

As long as the test audio was added in a supported format such as **MP3** or **WAV**, you can play it inside the app.

:::tip
Wear headphones if you want a more exam-like practice session.
:::

## Question types you may see

Listening tests can include question types such as:

- **fill-in-the-blank**
- **multiple choice**
- **matching**
- **map labelling**

For fill-in questions, watch for the small word-limit note under the answer box. For example, it may say **"no more than two words"**.

## A normal section-by-section flow

1. Start the test.
2. Play the audio for **Section 1**.
3. Answer the questions while listening.
4. Click **Submit & Continue** to unlock the next section.
5. Repeat this for Sections 2, 3, and 4.
6. After the last section, the app shows your results.

## How your band score is calculated

When the test ends, the app checks how many answers were correct and converts that total into an estimated IELTS band score on the **0-9 scale**.

Your results show:

- your overall **Band Score**
- your **total correct answers**
- a **section-by-section breakdown**

## Reviewing your answers

After finishing, you can open **Review Answers**.

This shows:

- the answer you gave
- whether it was correct or incorrect
- the correct answer for mistakes

That makes it easier to see whether your problems came from one section or from one question type.
