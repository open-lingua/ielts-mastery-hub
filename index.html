Get started

Up and running in two minutes

Three steps from download to your first band-score estimate.

01

Download

Grab the latest release for your OS from the GitHub releases page. macOS, Windows, and Linux builds are available.

$ brew install ielts-mastery-hub
02

Configure

Drop in your Anthropic API key and you're ready for AI-graded writing feedback. Everything else works offline.

ANTHROPIC_API_KEY=sk-ant-…
03

Practice

Work through real IELTS tasks, get instant AI feedback on your writing, and watch your band score rise week by week.
